Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What we collect

During the current demo phase, Rattle Empire stores data only in your own browser (local storage): your cart contents and your theme preference. No personal data is transmitted to our servers.

2. What we will collect in production

  • Account details you provide (email, password hash).
  • Order records required for delivery and receipts.
  • Seller verification documents, handled confidentially.

3. What we never do

  • We never sell personal data to third parties.
  • We never store full payment card details; payments are processed by licensed payment providers.
  • We never track you across other websites.

4. Your controls

You can clear your cart and theme preference at any time from your browser settings. In production you will be able to export or delete your account data by contacting support.

5. Contact

Privacy questions: support@rattleempire.example (production contact will be published at launch).
